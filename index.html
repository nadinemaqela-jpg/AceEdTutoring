How AceEd Works

We keep things simple, transparent, and tailored to your needs.

1. Book an Individual Lesson

Need help with a specific topic or an upcoming test? Browse our Tutors page, select a tutor, and click "Hire the Tutor". You can book a once-off, 1-on-1 session directly through their profile.

2. Request a Long-Term Tutor (Matching)

Looking for consistent, monthly support? Create a Parent Account, log in to your Dashboard, add your child's details, and submit a request. We will match you with the perfect tutor for ongoing sessions.

3. Make a General Enquiry

Not sure where to start or have a specific question? Fill out our Enquiry Form and our team will get back to you within 24 hours to guide you.

Important Notice: Parental Consent Required

All bookings and enquiries must be made by a parent or legal guardian. If a booking is made by a high school learner without parental consent, the session will be immediately voided and cancelled. We take the safety and financial security of our families very seriously.

About AceEd Tutoring

Founded in 2026, AceEd Tutoring is all about helping students do better in school and feel more confident in their studies. We understand how challenging the CAPS curriculum can be, so we offer personalized support to help every student reach their full potential.

Our goal is simple: to make quality education accessible and affordable for students across South Africa. Whether you need a quick refresher before a test or consistent help throughout the year, we are here for you.
